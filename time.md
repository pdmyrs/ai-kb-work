## Vacation Rules
- **Carryover limit:** Up to **40 hours** of unused vacation can roll over into the next calendar year.
- **Use-it-or-lose-it:** Any vacation hours beyond the 40-hour carryover cap that remain unused at year-end are forfeited — they do **not** carry forward.
- **Current year:** 2026

## Calendar View Rule
- The **Calendar View** section should never expand any earlier than the week containing **Thanksgiving** of the current year — regardless of other vacation days planned earlier in the year (e.g., Oct 23). Those earlier days are tracked in the **Plans** table and reflected in the **Current Balances**, but are not shown on the calendar grid.
